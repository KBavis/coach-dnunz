    There are two ways to add a VOD, both on the left:

    1. Drop the video file (.mp4 / .mkv / .mov / .webm) onto the dashed box. Best quality, and works offline.
    2. Paste a YouTube link and press Add. The video is downloaded for you first.

    Processing takes a while for long recordings. You can keep browsing other VODs meanwhile, and everything is saved to the library.
